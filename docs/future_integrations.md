# Future Integrations

The MVP intentionally avoids networked, paid, analytics, and authentication services. Once the local-first product is validated, these integrations can be added behind repository interfaces:

- Firebase Authentication for email, Google, and Apple sign-in.
- Firestore or Supabase cloud sync for multi-device habits and completion history.
- Local and push notifications for habit reminders.
- RevenueCat subscription entitlements for premium analytics or coaching.
- AdMob for free-tier monetization.
- Firebase Analytics or PostHog for privacy-aware product analytics.
- Cloud Functions for streak repair, scheduled nudges, and exports.
- Apple Health and Google Fit for health habit verification.
